About Town of Example

Town of Example is a small town government serving its residents and businesses. The Town Board meets twice a month at Town Hall, and meetings are open to the public.

This site is kept up to date by the Town Clerk. If you need help finding something, call the Clerk's office at (555) 000-0100.

Accessibility statement

Town of Example aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. If you find a barrier using this site, tell us what page you were on and what happened, by emailing accessibility@example-ny.gov or calling (555) 000-0000. If you cannot use the online form or this website at all, you can reach the Town at (555) 000-0000 or in person at 1 Example Plaza, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
